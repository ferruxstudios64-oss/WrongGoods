YOUR DETAILS.

This page covers the WrongGoods prelaunch landing page. We are developing fictional brands and graphic assets; products are not currently available to buy here.

What we collect

If you choose to join the release list, we save your email address, the wording of your consent, and when you signed up. We use this list to send WrongGoods release news. Signing up does not create an account or place an order.

Changing your mind

You can withdraw your consent or request removal at any time by emailing hello@wronggoods.com. Each release email will include a way to unsubscribe. Use the same email address you signed up with so we can locate your request.

Where the information goes

The landing page and its private signup database are hosted by Cloudflare. Resend processes your email address to maintain the launch-updates list and deliver confirmation and release emails. Only authorised WrongGoods administration can access the list. These providers also process technical request information needed to operate and secure the service. The form uses temporary hashed request identifiers to limit abuse; it does not store raw IP addresses in the signup table.

Contact

Email links open your own email application. If you contact us, we use the information you send to respond to your enquiry. For questions about your information, access or deletion, contact hello@wronggoods.com. Do not send payment-card information.

Cookies and external links

This page does not set advertising cookies or require a customer login. Instagram operates under its own privacy policy. If the release form cannot save your request, it shows an error instead of confirming a signup.
